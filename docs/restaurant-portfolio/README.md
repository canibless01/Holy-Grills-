## What to send a prospect

Send the PowerPoint or PDF. Use the Word document for your outreach message and walkthrough. The internal scope note is for planning; it is not a client proposal or quotation.

The slides show the existing Holy Grills implementation with a read-only, anonymised snapshot. Customer contact details and order identifiers are masked. Historical paid orders are used; no active kitchen queue or ingredient balances were invented. The deck distinguishes existing capabilities from the adaptation needed for another restaurant.

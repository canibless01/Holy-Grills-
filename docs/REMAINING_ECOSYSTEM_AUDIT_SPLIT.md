## Workstream A — this existing chat

This chat keeps the domains where it already has transaction, role and browser context:

1. **Wallet, funding and payment conservation.** Finish provider initialization/verification/webhook loops, signature and replay boundaries, mixed wallet/card payments, failed/cancelled callbacks, competing spends, equal-value independent intents, retries and recovery. Extend partial-refund/HP/benefit reconciliation and the financial reporting implications. Existing successful atomic-order/refund/credit tests are regression anchors, not full-provider sign-off.
2. **Order admission and fulfilment.** Finish guest and authenticated checkout, scheduled windows and consent, kitchen hours/date overrides, order-lock discounts/reminders/payment/fulfilment, stock/slot contention, guest tracking/token privacy and order QR flows. Extend bulk preparation, batch assignment, rider reassignment/pickup/delivery, duplicate/stale transitions and failure recovery beyond the verified single delivery.
3. **Rewards connected to orders.** Finish every spin category through consumption/fulfilment: physical delivery choices and attachment to the next order; delivery-fee caps; queued/overlapping awards; multiplier/discount/promo stacking; cancellation/refund restoration; auxiliary HP reward recovery and monthly limits. The primary payout, atomic spin and snapshot tests already pass; later consumption is still open.
4. **HP, referrals, squads and retention jobs.** Complete milestones, challenge/streak/referral/squad benefits, redemption choices, visibility to kitchen/admin and the fulfilment loop. Finish HP multiplier/flash reward release gates, random planning, advance staff notices and user announcements, including disabled-state behavior and Celery execution. Finish cart nudges and their cart deep links. Keep spin visual redesign deferred; fix functional gaps and record design notes.
5. **Transactional communication.** Verify order/wallet/reward/retention push and email events, preference enforcement, deep links, duplicate suppression and worker/outage recovery. This chat owns shared delivery transport and transactional tasks; Workstream B owns the admin blast and preference interfaces as described below.
6. **Storefront/media completion.** Keep the current CMS/carousel/contact/share-template context. Complete real Cloudinary upload/display/replace/delete and successful image-share export once the runtime hosts are applied; finish newsletter provider delivery and worker execution. Audit residual CMS write-scope/bulk-sort concurrency and prove all release-gate consumers. No Supabase Storage or committed credential fallback.

## Workstream B — new chat

This chat receives the less-explored platform and service domains:

1. **Events and ticketing.** Audit native event/ticket-tier creation/edit/delete/publication, campus/global scope, every customer/guest purchase field, limits and overselling, payment state, tickets, check-in/QR scans, duplicate scans, cancellations, refunds and reporting. Event QR/check-in belongs here; order QR/delivery belongs to A. Reuse the existing core payment/credit functions; coordinate any shared webhook change.
2. **Catering.** Audit every request field and validation, guest/account submission, campus routing, admin/kitchen receipt, quote/status/update/cancellation actions, communication and the customer-facing outcome. Complete missing interfaces and transitions using the existing brand.
3. **Marketplace and subscriptions/codes.** Audit listings/items, images, availability, tier/access restrictions, ownership, trades/purchases, subscriptions/code issuance/redemption, expiry, concurrency, receipts and admin fulfillment. Own these domain services; reuse A's wallet/HP transaction infrastructure rather than introducing another ledger.
4. **Identity and administration.** Audit signup/verification/login/logout/reset, onboarding/gifts/badges, profile/settings, staff/user management, activation/suspension and role assignment. Exercise guest, student, rider, kitchen, campus admin and super-admin boundaries. Preserve signed-in campus binding and the catalogue/order/financial protections already verified. User-editable auth metadata must never grant roles.
5. **Campus and academic setup.** Audit campus management, departments, levels, academic calendars, hostel/gate/delivery configuration and their respective admin fields. Prove each saved value is consumed by the appropriate page/API/job. Scope public contact WhatsApp to campus and global email to the general setting. A owns kitchen opening/closing override behavior against checkout; coordinate shared settings or delivery-fee changes.
6. **Inventory, operations and reporting interfaces.** Audit the remaining Store/Stock, operational dashboards, analytics, leaderboard administration, review moderation, system/webhook diagnostics and remaining sidebar pages. Verify read values against controlled actions, filters, pagination, exports, empty/error/retry states and permissions. A owns order admission and financial arithmetic; this workstream owns the administrative controls/reporting consumers.
7. **System settings, admin blasts and notification preferences.** Inventory which settings are actually read, their campus/global ownership, dead controls and missing fields. Audit blast targeting, previews, counts, authorization, duplicates, scheduling/cancellation and user receipt/deep links; audit preference inputs and persistence. Reuse shared transport/tasks owned by A and report needed shared changes. Existing feature-flag mutation/recovery UI is already covered; A owns deeper reward/event gates.
8. **Historical database/security inventory.** Review remaining RLS, grants, SECURITY DEFINER functions, views, triggers and unused schema objects with dependency and role tests. Advisor notices remain for public helpers, pg_net, newsletter-delivery policies and password protection. Harmless intentional helpers may need documented access rather than indiscriminate revocation. Do not delete a table/function/column just because one route does not use it; inspect code, SQL dependencies and jobs and prove a safe replacement first.

## Reporting defects corrected

- Economics overview and per-tier revenue previously summed original food subtotal, overstating retained food revenue after discounts/refunds. Both now use discounted paid food revenue, excluding delivery, with refunds allocated proportionately.
- Sales payment-channel totals previously subtracted refunds by their destination. A card purchase refunded into a member's wallet could leave card revenue unchanged. Retained revenue is now attributed to original wallet/card funding proportions; integer-cent rounding conserves the remaining total.
- Location, department/faculty/level, channel and academic-calendar revenue previously summed full order totals, including unpaid/preparing orders and amounts later refunded. Revenue now recognizes paid delivered orders after refunds. Their non-cancelled order counts retain the existing demand definition; chart copy makes that distinction explicit.
- Item revenue previously used gross price × quantity. It now allocates retained discounted food revenue over frozen line totals, with deterministic largest-remainder cents. Zero-priced physical rewards get zero revenue. The allocation conserves the order's food amount without counting delivery as menu sales.
- Order CSV preserves original charged fields and adds `card_refund_total`, `wallet_refund_total`, `retained_paid_amount`, `retained_food_amount` and `delivered_order_revenue`. Paid-but-not-delivered money is distinguished from delivered revenue.

These are reporting corrections, not new refund/payment policies. Refunds are not itemized by food/delivery line, so proportional attribution is an accounting allocation, matching the existing delivery cushion ledger; it does not claim to know which exact item a customer was refunded for. The included cushion remains part of retail food revenue, not an additional sale. No customer order, price, wallet, reward balance or provider transaction is edited. No database migration is needed. Existing date and campus filters and super-admin test exclusions remain in place. No new scheduled job, service or student-page request is added.
